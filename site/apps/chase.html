Known issues and caveats

Common questions

Does Chase work with ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT Finances (built in, powered by Plaid, US only). Read-only.

Does Chase work with Claude?

Partly. No official Claude connector for this institution. Reachable only via third-party connectors such as Truthifi (vendor-reported coverage).

Does Chase work with Grok?

Partly. Grok Bot 'Finance' integration (Plaid, read-only) announced Sep 26, 2026. No supported-institution list published.

Can an AI trade or move money in Chase?

Can look at balances and transactions. Cannot move money or trade. Read-only through ChatGPT Finances and Grok Finance. Neither can move money or pay bills.
