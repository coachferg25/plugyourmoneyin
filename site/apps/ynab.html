Does YNAB work with ChatGPT, Claude and Grok?

YNAB's official ChatGPT app gives budgeting guidance but cannot see your plan. Live-data access only through third-party MCP servers.

Read-only or can it trade?

Read + edit The official ChatGPT app has no access to your data. Third-party MCP servers can typically read and edit your budget.

Known issues and caveats

  • Third-party servers need a YNAB personal access token. You are trusting that server's author with it.
  • Verify any third-party server's code before use.

Common questions

Does YNAB work with ChatGPT?

Partly. Official YNAB app in ChatGPT is advice-only and can't see your plan. Third-party MCP servers can.

Does YNAB work with Claude?

Partly. No official YNAB MCP found. Third-party MCP servers exist (use your own API token).

Does YNAB work with Grok?

Unknown. Nothing found.

Can an AI trade or move money in YNAB?

Can read, and may be able to change data inside the app (not move money). The official ChatGPT app has no access to your data. Third-party MCP servers can typically read and edit your budget.
