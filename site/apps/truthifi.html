Common questions

Does Truthifi work with ChatGPT?

Partly. Supported, but Truthifi's own pages disagree on required ChatGPT plans (Plus/Pro on one page; Business/Enterprise/Edu on another).

Does Truthifi work with Claude?

Yes. Truthifi's guides list Claude (Pro, Max, Enterprise).

Does Truthifi work with Grok?

Partly. Truthifi guide pages mention Grok; its main supported-assistants list does not.

Can an AI trade or move money in Truthifi?

Can look at balances and transactions. Cannot move money or trade. Truthifi describes read-only access. Coverage claims are the vendor's own.
