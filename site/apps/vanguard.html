Common questions

Does Vanguard work with ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT Finances (built in, powered by Plaid, US only). Read-only. Plaid's Vanguard page lists Investments support.

Does Vanguard work with Claude?

Partly. No official connector; Truthifi lists Vanguard (vendor-reported).

Does Vanguard work with Grok?

Partly. Grok Bot 'Finance' integration (Plaid, read-only) announced Sep 26, 2026. No supported-institution list published.

Can an AI trade or move money in Vanguard?

Can look at balances and transactions. Cannot move money or trade. Read-only. Neither ChatGPT Finances nor Grok Finance can place trades.
