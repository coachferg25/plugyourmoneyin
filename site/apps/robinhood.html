Does Robinhood work with ChatGPT, Claude and Grok?

Robinhood has an official 'Agentic' setup that lets an outside AI agent trade through a dedicated account.

Read-only or can it trade?

Can trade The agent can place trades, but only inside the dedicated Agentic account you fund. Robinhood's docs say the agent can read your other accounts.

Known issues and caveats

  • Trading is limited to the separate Agentic account; read access spans your other accounts, so review what you share.
  • Requires an eligible Robinhood account; check Robinhood's page for current availability.
