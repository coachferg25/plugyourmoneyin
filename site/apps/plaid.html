Does Plaid work with ChatGPT, Claude and Grok?

Plaid is the pipe behind ChatGPT Finances and Grok's Finance integration. Its own Claude-compatible MCP is for developers, not for linking your accounts.

Read-only or can it trade?

Read-only Consumer links through ChatGPT/Grok are read-only. The Dashboard MCP is for developers' own Plaid teams.

Known issues and caveats

  • Dashboard MCP is 'under active development' with limited support, per Plaid.
  • Banks control what they share; Chase and others have said they restrict credential-based agents but still support Plaid.

Common questions

Does Plaid work with ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT Finances uses Plaid. Read-only.

Does Plaid work with Claude?

No. Plaid's remote Dashboard MCP exposes developer diagnostics (Link analytics, usage), not consumer bank data. No Claude consumer bank-linking feature has launched.

Does Plaid work with Grok?

Yes. Grok Bot's Finance integration connects through Plaid (read-only), per official @bot posts as reported.

Can an AI trade or move money in Plaid?

Can look at balances and transactions. Cannot move money or trade. Consumer links through ChatGPT/Grok are read-only. The Dashboard MCP is for developers' own Plaid teams.
