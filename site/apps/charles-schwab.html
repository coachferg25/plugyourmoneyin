Schwab says it is piloting an official MCP connection for traders, starting with Claude. Today there is no public self-serve connector.

Read-only or can it trade?

Read-only The CEO said connected models cannot move money. Community-built servers that use Schwab's Trader API can place orders and are not endorsed by Schwab.

Known issues and caveats

  • Pilot permissions have not been published.
  • Schwab says it blocks AI agents that log in with a client's username and password.
  • Community MCP servers need your own Schwab developer app and are unofficial.

Common questions

Does Charles Schwab work with Grok?

Unknown. No Schwab connector announced for Grok.

Can an AI trade or move money in Charles Schwab?

Can look at balances and transactions. Cannot move money or trade. The CEO said connected models cannot move money. Community-built servers that use Schwab's Trader API can place orders and are not endorsed by Schwab.
