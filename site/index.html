Can I plug my money into my AI?

Which US banks, brokerages and money apps actually work with ChatGPT, Claude and Grok, and whether they can only look or can also trade.

27 apps checked against their own help pages and docs. Last full check Oct 31, 2026. We say Unknown when we can't confirm, never a guess.

Quick answer. ChatGPT (Finances) and Grok (Finance integration) can link US bank, card and investment accounts read-only through Plaid. Claude has no built-in bank linking yet; it relies on company connectors (MCP) and third-party tools. Brokerages like Robinhood, Public and IBKR are the ones that can also trade.
