What changed

A dated log of changes to what works with ChatGPT, Claude and Grok. Newest first.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Version 1 launched with 27 apps

    First full check of banks, brokerages, budgeting apps and data aggregators. Every row shows its sources and the date it was checked.

  • Oct 29, 2026

    Monarch: connector docs still exclude Finicity-linked accounts

    Monarch's MCP help page was recently updated and still lists Finicity-sourced accounts as excluded.

    App: Monarch Money · Source

  • Oct 16, 2026

    Citi and Bank of America reported to be limiting AI agents

    The Financial Times reported both banks are restricting agent access to accounts. We have not found statements from either bank.

    App: Citi · Source

  • Oct 15, 2026

    Schwab: CEO describes pilot MCP access starting with Claude

    On its Q3 call Schwab said clients can connect their own model through a supervised pilot, starting with Claude, and that connected models cannot move money. Permissions were not published.

    App: Charles Schwab · Source

  • Oct 13, 2026

    Chase: blocks agents that log in with your password, still supports Plaid

    JPMorgan said it blocks outside AI agents that sign in using customer credentials. Customer-approved sharing through providers like Plaid remains supported. Reported via CNBC, relayed by a secondary source.

    App: Chase · Source

  • Sep 26, 2026

    Grok: Finance integration announced

    xAI's official @bot account announced a Finance integration that links bank, card and investment accounts through Plaid, read-only. No institution list was published.

    App: Plaid · Source

  • Sep 16, 2026

    Claude: 'Money' section spotted in testing

    BleepingComputer reported Anthropic testing a bank-linking feature in the Claude iOS app. Not launched, no bank list, no date.

    App: Plaid · Source
